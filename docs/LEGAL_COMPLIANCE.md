# Outlet Window - Legal and Compliance Requirements

## 1. Purpose
Define the legal, regulatory, privacy, and operational compliance requirements for Outlet Window in every active market.

## 2. Market Scope
- Initial pilot: Jubail, Saudi Arabia.
- National expansion: Saudi Arabia.
- International expansion: country-by-country approval.
- Each market must have a documented legal readiness assessment before launch.

## 3. Company and Intellectual Property
- Establish the appropriate legal entity before commercial operations.
- Confirm company ownership of source code, designs, domains, documentation, and intellectual property.
- Require written intellectual property assignment from developers and contractors.
- Maintain company-controlled accounts and access records.

## 4. E-commerce and Consumer Protection
- Publish clear terms of service and privacy notices.
- Display product prices, platform fees, delivery charges, taxes, and other costs before payment.
- Define cancellation, return, refund, and complaint processes in accordance with applicable law.
- Do not use AI to automatically remove or restrict statutory consumer rights.
- Keep evidence of customer consent and order confirmations.

## 5. Payments and Financial Compliance
- Use appropriately licensed payment service providers.
- Determine the merchant of record and invoicing responsibility for each market.
- Review requirements before holding, pooling, or transferring merchant funds.
- Do not operate an unlicensed wallet or payment service.
- Maintain auditable payment, refund, settlement, and reconciliation records.

## 6. Privacy and Personal Data
- Identify the applicable data protection laws in each market.
- Collect only data required for defined purposes.
- Obtain valid permissions for location, camera, and other device capabilities.
- Define retention, deletion, access, correction, and data export procedures.
- Assess cross-border data transfers before enabling them.
- Protect customer, merchant, shopper, and courier data with access controls and encryption.

## 7. Workforce and Delivery Compliance
- Review local requirements for couriers, shoppers, independent contractors, and employees.
- Verify eligibility, permits, insurance, and contractual obligations where required.
- Do not allow workers to perform tasks that conflict with their employment duties or working hours.
- Define health, safety, incident, and insurance procedures.

## 8. Merchant and Product Compliance
- Onboard merchants under written agreements.
- Verify merchant identity and business eligibility as required.
- Define responsibility for product authenticity, descriptions, pricing, stock, warranties, and recalls.
- Restrict prohibited or regulated products according to local law.

## 9. AI Governance
- Maintain human approval for legally significant or high-impact decisions.
- Log AI recommendations and actions.
- Do not allow agents to grant themselves permissions or bypass approval controls.
- Test for privacy, security, bias, and unsafe automated decisions.
- Provide escalation to a qualified human reviewer.

## 10. Insurance, Liability, and Safety
- Assess platform, merchant, courier, shopper, product, and cyber liability.
- Define incident reporting and claims handling.
- Review insurance requirements in each market.

## 11. Country Launch Checklist
Before launch in each country:
- Legal entity and licensing review.
- E-commerce and consumer protection review.
- Payment and tax review.
- Privacy and data transfer review.
- Workforce and transport review.
- Product category restrictions review.
- Contracts and insurance review.
- Local terms, policies, and customer support readiness.

## 12. Governance
- Assign a responsible compliance owner.
- Maintain a legal obligations register.
- Record legal advice, approvals, and review dates.
- Reassess requirements when entering a new market or adding a regulated service.

## 13. Important Limitation
This document is a product and engineering requirements framework, not legal advice or proof of regulatory approval. Qualified local legal and compliance professionals must verify requirements before launch.
