# Outlet Window - Cost and Delivery Estimate

## 1. Purpose
Establish a transparent framework for estimating Phase 0, development, launch, and operating costs.

## 2. Cost Categories
- Product discovery and system architecture.
- UX/UI design and prototyping.
- Backend and API development.
- Customer mobile application.
- Merchant portal.
- Shopper application.
- Courier application.
- Admin and Owner Control Center.
- AI agents and automation infrastructure.
- QA, security, and independent technical review.
- Cloud hosting, databases, storage, monitoring, and backups.
- Payment, maps, messaging, identity, and third-party integrations.
- Legal, regulatory, insurance, and business setup costs.
- Customer support and operational staffing.
- Marketing, onboarding, and merchant acquisition.

## 3. Development Team Model
Use the approved hybrid delivery model:
- Founder: product ownership and business decisions.
- Internal technical lead: architecture and technical oversight.
- External development team: implementation.
- Independent QA and security review.
- AI agents: engineering assistance, analysis, monitoring, and reporting under governance controls.

## 4. Product Scope for Estimation
Estimate each application and shared system separately:
- Customer App.
- Merchant Portal.
- Shopper App.
- Courier App.
- Admin Portal.
- Owner Control Center.
- Central AI Command and Operations System.
- Order, inventory, payment, logistics, handover, and notification services.

## 5. Operating Cost Model
Track recurring costs by:
- Cloud compute and networking.
- Database and backups.
- Storage and media processing.
- AI model usage and agent orchestration.
- Maps, routing, and geolocation.
- SMS, email, and messaging.
- Payment gateway fees.
- Monitoring and security services.
- Customer support and operations.
- Software subscriptions and vendor services.

## 6. Business Scenarios
Prepare separate estimates for:
- Phase 0 planning.
- MVP development.
- Pilot launch in Jubail.
- Expansion across Saudi Arabia.
- International expansion.

## 7. Financial Scenarios
Prepare conservative, expected, and high-growth scenarios. For each scenario estimate:
- Monthly orders.
- Average order value.
- Revenue per order.
- Merchant fees and delivery revenue.
- Shopper and courier compensation.
- Payment and refund costs.
- AI and infrastructure cost per order.
- Customer acquisition cost.
- Gross margin and contribution margin.
- Monthly operating expenses and cash runway.

## 8. Cost Governance
- Obtain vendor quotations before committing to material spending.
- Separate one-time setup costs from recurring costs.
- Track actual versus forecast costs.
- Require approval for spending above company-defined limits.
- Set budget alerts for cloud and AI usage.
- Review unit economics before opening a new market.

## 9. Estimation Rules
- Do not treat planning assumptions as confirmed vendor prices.
- Do not invent quotations or guaranteed delivery dates.
- Document assumptions, source, date, currency, and confidence level.
- Include taxes and payment fees where applicable.
- Identify costs that vary by country and regulatory requirements.

## 10. Approval Gates
- Phase 0 budget approval.
- MVP budget approval.
- Pilot operating budget approval.
- Expansion investment approval.

## 11. Status
This document defines the estimation framework. Final budgets require verified quotations, scope approval, and financial review.
