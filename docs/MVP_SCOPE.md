# Outlet Window - MVP Scope

## 1. MVP Objective
Launch a controlled first version in Jubail, Saudi Arabia, validating multi-merchant commerce, store inventory, shopping tasks, courier delivery, payments, and platform operations.

## 2. Customer Application
- Customer registration and secure login.
- Location permission and manual address selection.
- Merchant and store discovery.
- Product catalog with branch-level availability.
- Search, filters, favorites, and shopping cart.
- Multi-merchant checkout and order splitting.
- Delivery, scheduled delivery, and store pickup options.
- Payment, order tracking, support, and return requests.

## 3. Merchant Portal
- Merchant registration and branch management.
- Product and inventory management.
- Order receiving and preparation.
- Barcode, SKU, size, color, and quantity verification.
- POS, ERP, API, and CSV integration options.
- Order status, exceptions, reports, and financial settlements.

## 4. Shopper Application
- Independent shopper registration and eligibility verification.
- Mall Personal Shopper tasks.
- Store Employee order preparation for authorized stores.
- Mall Collector tasks.
- Scan & Shop support.
- Task offers, rewards, and acceptance.
- Product barcode scanning and item verification.
- Product, bag, and invoice evidence capture.
- Handover to courier or approved collection point.
- Earnings, task history, and performance reports.

## 5. Courier Application
- Courier registration and eligibility verification.
- Vehicle type and delivery capacity.
- Availability and service zones.
- Delivery offers, acceptance, and task management.
- Store and shopper pickup workflows.
- Route planning, GPS tracking during active tasks, and ETA.
- Pickup and delivery QR or one-time verification codes.
- Proof of delivery, earnings, and performance reports.

## 6. Unified Workforce and Task Management
- Central task marketplace for shoppers and couriers.
- Separate shopper and courier roles and permissions.
- Eligibility, assignment, availability, and reward rules.
- Task dependencies and handover management.
- Audit history and operational exception handling.

## 7. Core Commerce and Order Systems
- Multi-merchant cart and order splitting.
- Order and suborder lifecycle management.
- Inventory synchronization and availability confidence.
- Stock revalidation before order confirmation.
- Inventory reservation where supported.
- Pricing, fees, promotions, and transparent checkout.

## 8. Payments and Finance
- Approved payment gateway integration.
- Payment status and transaction records.
- Refund and cancellation workflows.
- Merchant settlements and courier earnings records.
- Financial ledger and reconciliation.
- Market-specific tax and compliance requirements.

## 9. Unified Handover and Invoice Intelligence
- Record every custody transfer.
- QR and one-time handover verification.
- Capture bag and invoice evidence.
- Extract invoice information using OCR.
- Compare ordered, paid, invoiced, and received items.
- Escalate discrepancies for human review.

## 10. Admin Portal
- Customer, merchant, shopper, and courier management.
- Orders, branches, service areas, and task monitoring.
- Support and exception management.
- Pricing and operational configuration.
- Reports, audit logs, and access control.

## 11. Owner Control Center
- Web dashboard and mobile application.
- Secure Telegram command bot.
- Central command and approval engine.
- AI agent monitoring and task management.
- Software development requests and release reviews.
- Security, infrastructure, financial, and business reports.

## 12. AI-First Capabilities
- AI Command Director and specialized agents.
- Proactive monitoring and issue detection.
- Development assistance and automated testing.
- Security and vulnerability analysis.
- Operations, finance, logistics, and business intelligence.
- Controlled safe remediation and human approval workflows.

## 13. Launch Scope and Future Expansion
The initial launch targets Jubail with selected cooperating merchants and verified workforce members. All core systems must be architected for multiple cities and countries.

Advanced features such as international shipping, loyalty membership, virtual try-on, advanced simulations, and large-scale marketing automation may follow after MVP validation.

## 14. Acceptance Requirements
- End-to-end customer order can be completed and tracked.
- Merchant can receive and prepare an order.
- Shopper can complete an assigned shopping task.
- Courier can collect and deliver an order.
- Handover records are verifiable and auditable.
- Payment and settlement records reconcile.
- Role permissions and security tests pass.
- Critical workflows have automated tests.
- Owner can monitor operations and review approvals.

## 15. Implementation Status
This document defines the proposed MVP scope. No application or service described here is implemented or operational yet. Detailed requirements, UX, APIs, architecture, cost estimates, and delivery plans must be completed during Phase 0.
