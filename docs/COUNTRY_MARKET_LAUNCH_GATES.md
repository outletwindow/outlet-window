# Outlet Window — Country & Market Launch Gates

Document ID: OW-MARKET-GATES-001
Version: 1.0
Status: Draft

## 1. Purpose
Define a controlled framework for launching Outlet Window in new countries, cities and regions.

## 2. Market Hierarchy
The platform supports:
- Country.
- State, province or region.
- City.
- District or service zone.
- Merchant.
- Store branch.
- Product category.

Each level can have independent service availability and configuration.

## 3. Market Readiness Assessment
Before launch, assess:
- Market demand and customer segments.
- Competitor landscape.
- Merchant availability and contracts.
- Delivery and logistics coverage.
- Payment provider availability.
- Currency and pricing requirements.
- Tax and invoicing obligations.
- Consumer protection requirements.
- Privacy and data protection laws.
- Workforce and contractor regulations.
- Returns and refunds requirements.
- Customer support language and hours.

## 4. Legal and Regulatory Gate
A market cannot launch until qualified reviewers assess:
- Company and operating permissions.
- E-commerce and marketplace requirements.
- Payment and settlement model.
- Consumer rights and disclosures.
- Tax and invoicing rules.
- Privacy and cross-border data transfers.
- Courier and shopper workforce requirements.
- Product category restrictions.
- Import, customs and shipping requirements where relevant.

## 5. Technical Readiness Gate
Confirm:
- Country configuration exists.
- Currency and localization are tested.
- Payment integrations are certified.
- Address and location services work.
- Delivery zones and pricing are configured.
- Merchant integrations are tested.
- Notifications and support are localized.
- Monitoring and incident response are operational.
- Data residency and backup requirements are reviewed.

## 6. Operational Readiness Gate
Confirm:
- Enough participating merchants.
- Verified store branches.
- Sufficient courier and shopper capacity.
- Defined service hours.
- Returns and dispute handling.
- Customer support coverage.
- Emergency contacts and escalation paths.

## 7. Feature Control
Features must be enabled through centralized controls by:
- Country.
- City.
- Service zone.
- Merchant.
- Branch.
- Category.
- Customer segment.

A feature unavailable or not approved in one market must remain disabled there.

## 8. Market Launch Stages
Stage 0: Research and feasibility.
Stage 1: Legal and commercial validation.
Stage 2: Technical integration and testing.
Stage 3: Internal pilot.
Stage 4: Limited customer pilot.
Stage 5: Controlled public launch.
Stage 6: Expansion and optimization.

Progression requires documented approval.

## 9. Pilot Limits
Each pilot must define:
- Geographic boundaries.
- Participating merchants.
- Supported categories.
- Maximum order volume.
- Operating hours.
- Support capacity.
- Financial exposure limits.
- Rollback conditions.

## 10. Market Performance
Monitor:
- Orders and completed deliveries.
- Cancellation and return rates.
- Delivery times.
- Merchant fulfillment accuracy.
- Customer satisfaction.
- Contribution margin.
- Payment failures.
- Support resolution times.
- Security and compliance incidents.

## 11. Rollback and Suspension
The company must be able to:
- Pause new orders.
- Disable a service or category.
- Suspend a merchant or branch.
- Restrict a geographic zone.
- Stop a payment or logistics integration.
- Preserve existing order and customer obligations.
- Communicate service interruptions.

Suspension must not improperly remove existing customer rights.

## 12. Ownership
- Market Operations: local readiness.
- Legal & Compliance: regulatory review.
- Engineering: technical readiness.
- Finance: unit economics and settlement.
- Security & Privacy: security and data protection.
- Executive Owner: final launch approval.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria
A market launch requires:
- Completed readiness assessment.
- Documented legal review.
- Approved payment and settlement setup.
- Tested logistics and merchant operations.
- Privacy and security review.
- Tested customer support.
- Defined financial limits.
- Approved pilot and rollback plan.
- Recorded final approval.

## 14. Implementation Status
This document defines launch governance requirements. It does not represent authorization to operate in any country or confirmation that regulatory approvals have been obtained.
